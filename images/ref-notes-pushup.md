**1. START Position (High Plank)**
* **Body angle:** ~15° incline from heels to shoulders.
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (fully extended); Hips 180° (flat); Knees 180°; Wrists 90° (flat on floor) or 180° (neutral on handles).
* **Placements:** Hands directly beneath shoulders; feet hip-width apart on toes; elbows facing slightly backward; head neutral, gaze ~8 inches ahead.

**2. END Position (Bottom)**
* **Body angle:** ~5°–10° to floor (chest lowered within 2–3 inches of floor).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 90° (arrowhead shape, ~45° from torso); Hips 180°; Knees 180°; Wrists unchanged.
* **Placements:** Chest aligned directly between hands; feet anchored on ball of foot; forearms vertical; head stays neutral with spine.

**3. Best View & Fixed Elements**
* **Best view:** **Side view** (most effectively shows straight spine alignment and elbow depth).
* **Fixed parts:** Hand/handle position, toe contact point, and rigid straight line from heels through hips to head.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to Avoid Showing**
1. **Sagging hips** (lumbar hyperextension below shoulder level).
2. **Piked hips** (butt stuck up in the air forming a tent shape).
3. **Craning neck / drooping head** (chin reaching toward the floor instead of a neutral cervical spine).
