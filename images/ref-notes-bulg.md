### **Image 1: TOP Position (Standing/Start)**

* **Torso/Body Angle:** ~75–80° relative to the floor (slight forward lean from the hips).
* **Shoulder Angle:** ~170–180° extension (arms hanging straight down by the sides).
* **Elbow Angle:** ~170–180° (arms nearly straight).
* **Hip Angle (Front Leg):** ~165–170° (nearly fully extended).
* **Hip Angle (Back Leg):** ~140–150° (thigh angled slightly backward).
* **Knee Angle (Front Leg):** ~165–175° (almost fully extended, slight soft bend).
* **Knee Angle (Back Leg):** ~90° (bent underneath the body).
* **Head Position:** Neutral, looking straight ahead, inline with the torso.
* **Hands:** Relaxed by the sides, fingers extended downward.
* **Feet Placement:** 
  * Front foot flat on the floor, directly underneath/slightly forward of the hip.
  * Back foot resting toes-down on a flat exercise bench behind the body.

### **Image 2: BOTTOM Position (Deepest Point of Squat)**

* **Torso/Body Angle:** ~70–75° relative to the floor (maintained slight forward lean).
* **Shoulder Angle:** ~140–150° flexion (shoulders pulled back slightly as hands move to hips).
* **Elbow Angle:** ~70–80° (elbows bent sharply outward).
* **Hip Angle (Front Leg):** ~85–95° (deep flexion at the hip crease).
* **Hip Angle (Back Leg):** ~160–170° (thigh drops vertically toward the floor).
* **Knee Angle (Front Leg):** ~85–95° (shin roughly vertical, knee directly over the mid-foot).
* **Knee Angle (Back Leg):** ~80–90° (knee hovering just above or lightly touching the floor).
* **Head Position:** Neutral, looking straight ahead.
* **Hands:** Placed firmly on the hips/waist.
* **Feet Placement:** Same as Image 1 (front foot flat on floor, back toes anchored on the bench).

---

### **What Stays Fixed Between Positions**

1. **Front Foot Position:** The entire sole of the front foot remains firmly planted on the floor without shifting forward, backward, or lifting the heel.
2. **Back Foot Placement:** The ball of the back foot/toes remain in the exact same contact point on the top of the bench.
3. **Torso Lean Angle:** The relative angle of the spine/torso to the floor (~75°) stays consistent throughout the descent to protect the lower back and engage the glutes.

---

### **Top 3 Mistakes an Illustration Must NOT Show**

1. **Knee Caving In (Valgus Collapse):** The front knee dropping inward toward the midline instead of tracking directly over the second and third toes.
2. **Excessive Forward Torso Collapse:** Bending forward at the waist so the chest points toward the floor (resembling a good-morning instead of a vertical squat path).
3. **Front Heel Lifting:** The heel of the front foot rising off the floor, shifting all the body weight entirely onto the toes and overloading the knee joint.
