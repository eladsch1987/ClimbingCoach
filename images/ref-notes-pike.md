**1. START Position**
* **Body angle:** Inverted “V”; torso ~60° to the floor.
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (locked), Hips ~80°, Knees 180° (straight), Wrists ~90°.
* **Placement:** Hands shoulder-width on floor; feet hip-width elevated on toes; head neutral, aligned between arms, gazing toward feet.

**2. END Position (Bottom)**
* **Body angle:** Torso pitched forward ~45–50° to floor.
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 90° (forearms vertical); Hips ~75–80°; Knees 180°; Wrists ~90–100°.
* **Placement:** Head touches ground 6–8 inches *in front* of hands (tripod apex); elbows tucked 45° toward ribs; feet remain on tiptoes.

**3. Camera View & Fixed Points**
* **Best view:** **Side profile** (clearly shows forward head travel, hip hinge, and vertical forearms).
* **Fixed points:** Hand and toe placement points on the floor remain completely stationary.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to NOT Show**
1. **Head dropping between hands:** Lowering straight down instead of moving forward into a triangle/tripod.
2. **Flared elbows:** Elbows bowing 90° outward to the sides instead of pointing back.
3. **Sagging hips:** Opening the hip angle (hips dropping toward floor), turning it into a standard push-up.
