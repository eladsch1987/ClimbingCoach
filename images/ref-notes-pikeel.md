### What Stays Fixed Between Both Positions
* **Hand placement:** Palms do not shift or slide on the floor.
* **Foot placement:** Feet remain securely planted on top of the elevated blocks.
* **Leg straightness:** Knees stay fully locked with zero bend throughout the movement.

### Top 3 Mistakes an Illustration Must NOT Show
1. **Flared, wide elbows (Chicken Wings):** Elbows flaring straight out to the sides instead of tracking backward at a 45-degree angle relative to the torso.
2. **Sagging hips/banana back:** Hips dropping toward the floor, resulting in an arched lower back and a plank-like position instead of a strict pike.
3. **Bending the knees:** Allowing the knees to flex during the descent or push phase instead of keeping the legs completely straight.
