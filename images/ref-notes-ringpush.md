**1. START Position (Top plank)**
* **Body angle:** ~15°–20° incline to the floor.
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (fully extended), Hips 180° (straight line), Knees 180°, Wrists 180° (neutral, stacked over rings).
* **Positions:** Hands directly below shoulders; feet together on toes; elbows turned slightly outward/back; head neutral, eyes looking slightly forward.

**2. END Position (Bottom dip)**
* **Body angle:** ~5°–10° incline (parallel-adjacent).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows ~90°, Hips 180°, Knees 180°, Wrists 180° (neutral).
* **Positions:** Hands beside lower chest/ribs; feet remain anchored on toes; elbows tucked close to torso (pointing backward at 30°–45°); head neutral, aligned with spine.

**3. Best Camera View & Fixed Parts**
* **Camera view:** **Side view** (best shows elbow depth, body line, and ring position).
* **Fixed elements:** Foot contact point on the ground and the rigid line from heels to head (hips and knees stay locked at 180° throughout).

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to Avoid Showing**
1. **Sagging or piked hips:** Breaking the 180° torso-leg line (lumbar hyperextension or hips pushed up).
2. **Flared elbows:** Elbows jutting out at 90° perpendicular to the body.
3. **Bent wrists:** Wrists collapsing backward instead of staying rigid and neutral over the rings.
