**1. START Position (Scapular Protraction)**
* **Body angle to floor:** ~10°–15° incline from heels to shoulders.
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (straight), Hips 180°, Knees 180°, Wrists ~90°.
* **Placements:** Hands stacked directly under shoulders; feet hip-width on toes; elbows locked; head neutral (gaze directed at the floor). Upper back is actively pushed up toward the ceiling (shoulder blades apart).

**2. END Position (Scapular Retraction)**
* **Body angle:** ~10°–15° (rigid plank maintained).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (strictly unbent), Hips 180°, Knees 180°, Wrists 90°.
* **Placements:** Hands, feet, and head remain in place; chest drops 2–3 inches toward the floor, pinching the shoulder blades firmly together.

**3. Best View & Fixed Elements**
* **Best view:** Side (lateral) profile to clearly show the torso dip versus straight arms.
* **Fixed parts:** Elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and feet stay locked in position. Only the ribcage/scapulae move vertically.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes NOT to Show**
1. **Bent elbows** (<180°)—turning it into a standard push-up.
2. **Sagging lower back** (hips dropping out of the 180° line).
3. **Craning the neck/dropping the chin** to fake movement depth.
