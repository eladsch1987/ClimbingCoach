MODEL gemini-3.5-flash-lite
**Correct Support-Hold Position:**
* **Arm angle / Elbow lockout:** Arms vertical and fully locked out; elbows pointing straight back.
* **Shoulder position:** Depressed and strongly protracted (shoulders pushed down away from ears, chest hollowed slightly).
* **Body angle:** Vertical torso alignment directly stacked over the hands.
* **Hip / Knee position:** Hips neutral or slightly flexed; knees bent at roughly 90 degrees with feet tucked back.
* **Feet:** Pointed down and together behind the torso.
* **Head:** Neutral, looking straight ahead.
* **Hand position:** Hands gripping bars directly underneath or slightly behind the hip line.

**3 Mistakes to Avoid:**
1. Shrugged shoulders (shoulders sagging up toward the ears).
2. Bent elbows (arms partially flexing or soft lockout).
3. Piking excessively at the hips or swinging the legs forward.
