### 2. Position Specifications

#### **BOTTOM POSITION (00:39)**
*   **Body angle relative to the floor:** ~45 degrees diagonal.
*   **Straightness of head-hips-heels line:** Perfectly straight plank (rigid line from head through hips to heels).
*   **Feet/heels:** Heels resting on the floor (heels in contact, toes elevated/pointed upward).
*   **Knee angle:** 180 degrees (fully locked out/straight).
*   **Arm angle relative to the torso:** 90 degrees (arms extended straight up perpendicular to the angled torso).
*   **Elbow angle and direction:** 180 degrees (fully extended/straight arms); elbows pointing forward/downward along the line of the straps.
*   **Hand/ring position relative to the chest:** Arms fully extended away from the chest; rings held at arm's length overhead.
*   **Wrist/grip orientation:** Palms facing inward toward each other (neutral grip).
*   **Shoulder blades:** Depressed and retracted (shoulders pulled down away from ears, chest puffed out).
*   **Head/neck:** Neutral spine, looking up toward the anchor point on the ceiling.
*   **Ring height:** Straps hang vertically from anchor points; rings suspended at approximately belly-button height when standing, but positioned above the chest level relative to the extended body at this angle.

#### **TOP POSITION (00:31)**
*   **Body angle relative to the floor:** ~45 degrees diagonal (maintains the same plank angle as the bottom position).
*   **Straightness of head-hips-heels line:** Completely rigid, straight plank line maintained from head to heels with no sagging at the hips.
*   **Feet/heels:** Heels remaining on the floor in constant contact.
*   **Knee angle:** 180 degrees (legs remain fully straight).
*   **Arm angle relative to the torso:** Upper arms tucked close to the sides (elbows forming roughly a 0 to 30-degree angle relative to the torso).
*   **Elbow angle and direction:** Acute angle (~45 to 90 degrees at the elbow joint); elbows pointing straight down toward the floor.
*   **Hand/ring position relative to the chest:** Rings touching/pulled tightly into the sides of the ribcage / mid-chest.
*   **Wrist/grip orientation:** Palms facing each other (neutral grip).
*   **Shoulder blades:** Actively squeezed together and pulled down (away from ears).
*   **Head/neck:** Neutral, chin tucked slightly or aligned with the spine, eyes focused up toward the anchor point.
*   **Ring height:** Rings pulled up flush against the mid-chest/ribs.

### 3. Common Mistakes to AVOID in the Illustration
*   **Sagging hips / broken midsection:** Hips dropping toward the floor (looking like a "dead fish").
*   **Bent knees:** Knees flexing during the pull.
*   **Shrugging shoulders:** Shoulders hiked up around the ears instead of pulled down and back.
*   **Flaring elbows:** Elbows pointing outward to the sides (90 degrees away from the torso) instead of tracking close to the ribs.
*   **Incomplete range of motion:** Arms not fully locking out at the bottom or rings not touching the chest at the top.
