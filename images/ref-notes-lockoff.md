**1. START Position (Top Lock-Off)**
* **Body angle to floor:** ~80°–85° (slight hollow-body tilt).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 30°–45° (fully flexed); Wrists 180° (neutral); Hips 170°–180°; Knees 180° (straight).
* **Placement:** Hands shoulder-width on bar; chin clearly above the bar; elbows pulled down and close to ribs; head neutral (eyes forward); feet slightly in front of the vertical plane.

**2. END Position (90° Lock-Off)**
* **Body angle to floor:** 90° (vertical).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows exactly 90°; Wrists 180° (neutral); Hips 180°; Knees 180°.
* **Placement:** Hands fixed on bar; eyes/forehead level with bar; elbows at mid-chest height; feet vertically aligned below hips.

**3. Camera View & Fixed Parts**
* **Best view:** **Side profile** (best displays precise elbow angles and torso line).
* **Fixed parts:** Hand grip position, neutral wrist alignment, and actively depressed scapulae (shoulders down).

**4. Top 3 Mistakes NOT to Show**
1. **Shrugged shoulders:** Neck disappearing into the traps (must show active shoulder depression).
2. **Banana back:** Excessive lumbar hyperextension (hips arched backward >15°).
3. **Cocked wrists:** Wrists hyperextended under the bar rather than straight/neutral.
