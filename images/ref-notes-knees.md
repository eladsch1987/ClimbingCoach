**1. START Position**
* **Body angle:** 90° (perpendicular to floor).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (fully extended), Hips 180° (neutral), Knees 180° (straight), Wrists 180° (neutral).
* **Locations:** Hands overhand, shoulder-width on bar; elbows locked overhead; head neutral, facing forward; feet together suspended directly under hips.

**2. END Position**
* **Body angle:** Torso ~80°–90° to floor (slight posterior pelvic tilt).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (straight), Hips 70°–80° (thighs at or above parallel), Knees ~90°, Wrists 180°.
* **Locations:** Hands and elbows remain locked on bar; knees lifted to chest level; feet tucked directly below knees; head neutral.

**3. Best View & Fixed Parts**
* **Camera view:** **Side (profile) view**—best displays hip flexion depth, pelvis tuck, and lack of torso swing.
* **Fixed body parts:** Hands, wrists, elbows (arms stay completely straight), shoulders, and head.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to NOT Show**
1. **Bent elbows:** Pulling with arms instead of engaging the core.
2. **Swinging torso:** Legs driven by backward kipping momentum rather than strict abdominal contraction.
3. **Low knees:** Knees stopping below 90° hip flexion (thighs below parallel to floor).
