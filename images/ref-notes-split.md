**1. START Position**
* **Body angle:** 85°–90° to floor (upright torso, neutral spine).
* **Joint angles:** Front/back knees 170°–180° (extended, soft lock); hips 170°–180°; elbows and wrists 180° (fully extended, neutral).
* **Locations:** Head neutral, eyes forward. Hands hang straight down at sides holding weights. Feet staggered split: front foot flat on floor; back foot 2–3 feet back on the ball of the foot (toes tucked).

**2. END Position (Bottom)**
* **Body angle:** 75°–80° slight forward lean, matching shin angle.
* **Joint angles:** Front knee ~90° (shin near vertical); back knee ~90° (hovering 1–2 inches above ground); front hip ~90°; elbows and wrists 180°.
* **Locations:** Head neutral. Hands hang vertically at mid-thigh level. Front foot entirely flat; back foot anchored on ball of foot.

**3. Best View & Fixed Parts**
* **Camera view:** **Side profile** (clearly captures knee angles, shin angle, and depth).
* **Fixed parts:** Foot contact points/stance width, fully straight arms/wrists, and neutral spinal alignment.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes NOT to Show**
1. **Front heel lifting** off the floor (shifting weight forward onto toes).
2. **Torso collapsing forward** (rounding lower back, angle <65°).
3. **Stance too narrow**, causing the front knee to shoot excessively forward past toes.
