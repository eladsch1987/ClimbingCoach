Here is the exact side-view tutorial breakdown for the ring face pull:

### **START Position (Arms Straight)**
* **Body angle to floor:** ~60° to 65° lean back (straight line from heels through hips to shoulders).
* **Feet:** Heels on the floor, legs straight, toes pulled up.
* **Grip:** Palms facing down (pronated).
* **Ring height:** Suspended at upper-chest/shoulder height when arms are extended upward.

### **END Position (Pulled In)**
* **Elbow height:** Level with or slightly above shoulders, flared outward at 90° from the torso.
* **Hand position:** Hands level with ears/temples, fists facing forward.
* **External-rotation "goalpost":** Forearms rotated upward to form a 90° angle at the elbow (bicep horizontal, forearm vertical).
* **Shoulder blades:** Retracted and depressed (pinched together and pulled down).

---

### **Top 3 Common Mistakes**
1. **Elbow drop:** Pulling elbows down toward ribs (lat-dominant row) instead of flaring them high and back (rear delt/upper back focus).
2. **Incomplete external rotation:** Failing to rotate forearms up into the "goalpost" position at the top, missing the upper back/rotator cuff contraction.
3. **Core/Hip sag:** Allowing hips to drop or lower back to arch excessively during the pull, breaking core tension.
