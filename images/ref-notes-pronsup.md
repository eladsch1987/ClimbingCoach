**1. START Position**
* **Torso & Head:** Upright (90° to floor); head neutral, looking forward.
* **Hips & Knees:** Flexed at 90°; feet flat on floor.
* **Working Arm:** Elbow flexed at ~90°; entire forearm resting flat on table with wrist extending just past the edge.
* **Hand & Wrist:** Wrist neutral (0° flexion/deviation); holding bottom end of dumbbell vertically (90° to table). Free hand rests on thigh.

**2. END Position (Supination)**
* **Torso, Legs & Head:** Identical to start (torso upright 90°, hips/knees 90°).
* **Working Arm:** Elbow and forearm remain anchored flat to table.
* **Hand & Wrist:** Forearm rotated 90° outward; palm facing ceiling; dumbbell horizontal (0°/parallel to table); wrist remains rigid and straight (0° flexion/extension).

**3. Best View & Fixed Parts**
* **Best View:** **Front view** (best shows the 90° rotational arc of the hammer-grip dumbbell and palm orientation).
* **Fixed Parts:** Head, torso, shoulders, feet, non-working arm, and the elbow/forearm contact point on the table.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes NOT to Show**
1. Elbow or forearm lifting off the tabletop.
2. Wrist flexing, extending, or twisting instead of pure forearm rotation.
3. Torso swaying or shoulder tilting to swing the weight.
