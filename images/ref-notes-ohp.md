**1. START Position**
* **Body angle:** 90° (vertical to floor).
* **Joint angles:** Pressing elbow ~45°–60° flexion; non-working elbow ~170°–180°; hips 180°; knees 180° (extended); wrists 180° (neutral, stacked).
* **Placements:** Feet shoulder-width apart, flat. Working hand holds dumbbell at shoulder/ear height. Pressing elbow vertically under wrist, angled ~30° forward in scapular plane. Head neutral, facing forward. Non-working arm relaxed at side.

**2. END Position**
* **Body angle:** 90° vertical.
* **Joint angles:** Pressing elbow 180° (full extension); hips 180°; knees 180°; wrists 180° (straight).
* **Placements:** Working hand vertically locked out directly above shoulder and midfoot. Feet, non-working arm, and head remain identical to start.

**3. Best View & Fixed Parts**
* **View:** **Front view** (best displays unilateral balance, elbow path, and torso stability).
* **Fixed parts:** Feet, legs, hips, spine/torso, and non-working arm remain completely static.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to Avoid**
1. **Torso lean:** Lateral spine bending to counterbalance the dumbbell or excessive lower back arching.
2. **Bent wrist:** Wrist hyperextending backward instead of staying neutral beneath the handle.
3. **Knee dip:** Bending knees to push-press rather than pressing strictly from the shoulder.
