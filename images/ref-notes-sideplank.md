**1. START Position (Side-lying prep)**
* **Body angle:** 0°–10° (hips and legs resting on floor).
* **Joint angles:** Bottom elbow 90°; hips 180° (extended); knees 180° (straight); wrists neutral.
* **Placements:** Bottom elbow directly beneath the shoulder, forearm flat on mat; feet stacked (or staggered heel-to-toe); top hand on hip; head neutral, aligned with spine.

**2. END Position (Elevated hold)**
* **Body angle:** 20°–30° diagonal line from feet to shoulders.
* **Joint angles:** Bottom elbow 90°; hips 180° (fully open/extended); knees 180°; top shoulder abducted to 180° (top arm vertical); wrists neutral.
* **Placements:** Bottom elbow locked directly under shoulder; body supported on outer edge of bottom foot and forearm; top arm pointing straight to ceiling; head centered along spinal axis.

**3. Best View & Fixed Points**
* **Camera view:** Frontal view (facing the torso perpendicularly, as shown) to capture hip lift and vertical limb alignment.
* **Fixed points:** Bottom elbow, bottom forearm, and feet stay planted as pivot anchors.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to Avoid**
* Sagging hips (breaking the 180° line between ankles and shoulders).
* Elbow placed too far forward/out from under the shoulder joint.
* Torso/hips rolling forward or backward instead of staying stacked vertically.
