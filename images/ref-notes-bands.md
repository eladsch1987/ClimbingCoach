**1. START Position**
* **Body angle:** Torso upright, 90° to floor (seated or standing).
* **Joint angles:** Hips/knees 90° (seated); elbow bent at 90°; wrist neutral (0° flexion/extension).
* **Placements:** Head neutral facing forward; feet flat on floor; elbow tucked near ribs/resting on table; hand at mid-chest level with fingertips gathered tightly into a closed cone shape (band taut around distal fingertips).

**2. END Position**
* **Body/joints:** Identical to START (torso 90°, elbow 90°, wrist strictly neutral at 0°).
* **Hand placement:** Fingers and thumb extended straight and flared outward into maximum spread (~45°–60° separation between digits), stretching the band fully.

**3. Camera View & Fixed Parts**
* **Best view:** Direct front view (centered on hand and forearm).
* **Fixed parts:** Torso, shoulders, elbow, forearm, and wrist remain completely stationary; only the finger/thumb joints move.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to NOT Show**
1. **Wrist hyperextension:** Wrist bending backward (>15° extension) to cheat the resistance.
2. **Incorrect band placement:** Band slipping down to the palm or lower knuckles instead of distal fingertips.
3. **Clawing fingers:** Fingertips curling inward into a claw shape rather than opening outward flat and wide.
