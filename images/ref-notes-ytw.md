**1. START Position**
* **Body angle:** Flat on floor (0°).
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (straight), hips 180°, knees 180°, wrists 180° (neutral).
* **Positions:** Forehead rests on pad/floor (neck neutral). Feet together, toes resting on floor. Arms extended overhead forming a “Y” (~30°–45° abduction from torso), hands resting lightly on the floor, thumbs pointing toward ceiling.

**2. END Position**
* **Body angle:** Flat on floor (0°); torso/pelvis remain anchored.
* **Joint angles:** Elbows 180° (straight), hips/knees 180°, wrists 180°.
* **Positions:** Arms lifted 15–20 cm (6–8 in) off the floor via mid/lower-trap squeeze. Thumbs point vertically toward ceiling (shoulder external rotation). Head and feet stay completely resting on the floor.

**3. Camera View & Fixed Parts**
* **Best view:** High front-quarter (front-3/4) angle to clearly capture the “Y” shape, thumbs-up grip, and arm elevation.
* **Fixed parts:** Forehead, chest, torso, hips, and feet remain fully grounded.

**4. Top 3 Mistakes to NOT Show**
* **Lumbar hyperextension:** Arching the lower back or lifting chest off the ground.
* **Craning the neck:** Lifting the head or looking forward instead of down.
* **Bent elbows / internally rotated shoulders:** Bending arms or pointing thumbs down/inward.
