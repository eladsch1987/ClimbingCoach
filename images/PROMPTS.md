# Exercise illustration prompts

Generate these with Dreamina (Seedream) on a machine that has the Dreamina CLI.
Save each image in this folder under the filename shown, as a 4:3 PNG of at least 1200 px wide.
After that, the coach session wires them into `workout-timer.html` and `exercise-guide.html`, replacing the stick figures, and republishes both pages to their existing URLs.

## Shared style (prepend to every prompt)
Clean instructional fitness illustration, flat vector style, one athletic man in his late 30s, short dark hair, plain grey t-shirt and dark shorts, barefoot, on a light grey exercise mat, plain off-white background, soft even lighting, accurate anatomy and joint angles, full body visible, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Hip mobility
| File | Prompt |
|---|---|
| `frog.png` | Frog stretch, three-quarter view from behind and slightly above. Resting on forearms and knees, knees spread very wide apart, shins pointing straight out to the sides, inner ankles in line with the knees, hips pushed back toward the heels, back flat, head relaxed. |
| `ninety.png` | 90/90 hip stretch, viewed from the front and slightly above. Sitting upright on the floor. Front leg: thigh pointing forward, knee bent 90 degrees, shin lying across in front of the body. Back leg: thigh pointing out to the side, knee bent 90 degrees, shin pointing backward. Hands lightly on the floor beside the hips, chest tall. |
| `ninety-switch.png` | Same man mid-transition of a 90/90 switch, seated, both knees lifted and pointing up, feet flat on the floor wider than hips, swinging the knees from one side to the other, hands behind him on the floor. |
| `couch.png` | Couch stretch, side view. Back knee on a folded cushion right against the front of a sofa, back shin vertical up against the sofa, top of the foot on the sofa. Front foot flat on the floor forward, front knee bent 90 degrees above the ankle. Torso upright, hands resting on the front knee. |
| `squat.png` | Deep bodyweight squat hold, side view. Feet shoulder-width, heels flat on the floor, hips sitting low below knee level, chest up, back long, elbows inside the knees with palms pressed together. |
| `pancake.png` | Seated pancake stretch, front view slightly above. Sitting with legs straight and spread wide in a V, toes pointing up, hinging forward from the hips with a long straight back, hands walked forward on the floor between the legs. |
