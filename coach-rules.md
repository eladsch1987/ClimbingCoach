## Progression gates (to advance a phase on schedule)
- **Into Phase 2:** 4 weeks with no finger/elbow pain >2/10, and morning-after symptoms back to baseline. Comfortable on V3s.
- **Into Phase 3:** density/repeater sessions pain-free for 3+ weeks; can hang bodyweight on 20 mm half crimp ~10 s; 8+ clean pull-ups before lock-offs.
- **3rd climbing day/week:** only if fingers quiet, sleep good (7+ h most nights), and performance not dropping.
- Gate not met → extend the current phase by one week, note it under State.
- Change **one variable at a time**: don't add a climbing day, a new hangboard protocol and more pull-up volume in the same week.

## Daily decision rules (apply in this order)
1. **Red flag** (pop, swelling, bruising, sharp localised finger pain, pain that changes how he grips) → no climbing/fingers; tell him to see a hand/climbing physio.
2. **Pain >2/10 during loading or not back to baseline next morning** → today: no fingers, no hard pulling. Next finger session one level lighter.
3. **Day after a hard climb or a MaxHang/density session** → no fingerboard, no pull-ups/lock-offs. Do: antagonists (push), legs, core (not hanging), shoulder health, wrist extensors, mobility. Or rest.
4. **Day before a climb** → no fingerboard beyond Abrahangs, no heavy pulling. Light push/legs/mobility or rest.
5. **Never two hard finger days in a row.** Hard finger day = limit/projecting climb, MaxHangs, density hangs, repeaters.
6. **Fingerboard goes before climbing, never after.** Keep board time under 20 min.
7. **Abrahangs** can be done on most days in Phase 1–2, but at least ~6 h away from climbing, and skip them the day after a very hard session if fingers feel worked.
8. **At 3 climbing days/week**, cut standalone pull-up volume by about half.
9. **Minimum effective dose on a bad week:** 1 climb + 3 × 10-min Abrahangs + 1 × 20-min park session.
10. **At least 1 full rest day per week.** Two if he's climbing 3×.

## Grip rules (first ~12 weeks)
No full crimp on the board, no two-finger pockets or monos, no edges <15 mm, no one-arm hangs, no campus board. Never hang to failure. Open hand and half crimp on ≥20 mm first.

## Session menu (details in `research/report.md` §B–D)
- **Climb – volume/technique:** warm-up 15–20 min → 4×4 or pyramid on V2–V3 + drills (silent feet, hover hands, down-climbing) → flash block → antagonist cool-down.
- **Climb – projecting:** warm-up → 3–5 problems at max, 3–5 min rest, ≤5–6 quality attempts each → flash block → cool-down.
- **Climb – once-a-week mixed:** warm-up → 25 min projecting → 15 min flashing → 15 min volume.
- **Park A (pull):** jump rope 3 min → scap pulls → pull-ups 5 sets (~50–70% of max reps) → ring rows 3×10 → tuck front lever 5×8 s → dips 3 sets → hollow hold 3×30 s → external rotations.
- **Park B (push/legs):** jump rope → ring push-ups 3 sets → pike push-ups 3 sets → DB overhead press 3 sets → lock-offs 3×3 holds (only once 8+ pull-ups) → hanging leg raises 3×8 → split squats 3×10 → Y-T-W + reverse wrist curls → hip mobility.
- **Recovery/antagonist (no grip):** jump rope easy → push-ups / pike push-ups / overhead press → split squats → hollow + side plank → external rotations, Y-T-W → reverse wrist curls, finger-extensor bands → 10-min hip mobility.
- **Abrahangs (10 min):** feet on floor, 10 × ~10 s hangs at effort 2–4/10, alternating half crimp / open hand / 3-finger drag, 20 mm edge.
- **Rest:** walk + optional 10-min mobility.

## Nutrition defaults
- ~1,850 kcal/day average: ~2,000 on climbing/strength days, ~1,750 on rest days. Never below ~1,750 average.
- Protein 140–160 g/day in 4 feedings of 35–40 g (vegetarian: skyr, cottage cheese, eggs, whey, tofu, tempeh, seitan, legumes).
- Carbs before and after climbing. Never train fasted.
- Every 2 weeks, check the 7-day average weight: <0.3 kg/week loss → −100–150 kcal; >0.8 kg/week or performance/sleep dropping → +150–200 kcal.
- Creatine 3–5 g/day (expect +0.5–1 kg water early); B12; vitamin D only if tested low.
- Consider a 1–2-week maintenance "diet break" around week 12 if energy, sleep or performance dip.

## Warning signs → back off
- Performance down 2+ sessions in a row, or benchmarks down >5%.
- Poor sleep, irritability, low motivation, elevated resting HR, constant hunger, feeling cold, frequent illness, weight dropping >1%/week.
- Response: deload week. If it persists: maintenance calories for 1–2 weeks; suggest a GP check (ferritin, B12, vitamin D).
