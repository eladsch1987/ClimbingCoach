Jump rope: build up, don't suffer

Three minutes straight is hard for anyone new to the rope. Your calves and Achilles also need time to adapt to barefoot jumping. So use intervals and build up:

  • Weeks 0–1: 30 s jump / 30 s rest × 4 (2 min of jumping). If 30 s is too much, do 20 s on / 40 s off.
  • Each week after: add 10 s to each jump block. Aim for 3 min straight around week 5–6.
  • Tripping is normal. Just restart. It doesn't count against you.
  • Rope length: stand on the middle of the rope; the handles should reach your armpits.
  • Technique: jump only 1–2 cm, land softly on the balls of your feet, knees soft, elbows close to your ribs, and turn the rope with your wrists, not your arms.
  • Barefoot: on rubber, wood or grass, not concrete. If your calves are still sore the next day or your Achilles is stiff in the morning, wear shoes or halve the volume.
  • To rest your legs without stopping: switch to the alternate-foot "jog step".
